How the model works

Three ports share a fleet of 150 containers. Loaded containers and repositioned empties arrive next week. Bookings that cannot be served expire. The two repositioning policies each have a weekly limit of 24 moves.

The fixed policy aims for 22 containers at each port, including arrivals already in transit. The forecast policy uses the previous three weeks of observed booking requests, including the week that just closed, and adds a buffer of five containers. Its chosen stock targets can cause it to move more containers even under the same weekly limit.

These scenarios are synthetic. Every port pair can exchange containers. The simulation has no lane-specific costs, terminal limits or carrier commitments.
